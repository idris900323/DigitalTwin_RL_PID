\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
In simulation on an unvalidated digital twin, a residual PPO around a validation-tuned constant beat that constant by about $0.5$--$0.6$ median return, but a much larger validation search for the constant recovers most of the gain and a simple non-learned load-conditioned rule matches the policy; a small residual advantage over the stronger constant remains in the static and out-of-distribution regimes ($\SensVThreeStaticPpoBestConstantExtMed$ and $\SensVThreeOodPpoBestConstantExtMed$ median return, about $\SensVThreeStaticPpoExtPct\,\%$ and $\SensVThreeOodPpoExtPct\,\%$ of $|$return$|$) and none when payload and target change; we do not attribute it to adaptation. An unconstrained setup and TD3 failed to beat the constant and SAC was mixed. The contribution is the evaluation protocol and the evidence that it matters. Next steps: a second motor model and more seeds (only worthwhile if a configuration beats the stronger baselines), reward-weight sensitivity, tuning the learner and the constant with matched budgets, relaxing the $K_d$ bound that the extended constants reach, and the hardware replication protocol of Appendix~\ref{app:hardware}, which would be the first test of any of these claims against measurements.
